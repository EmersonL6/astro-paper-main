  It's so weird to live in another language. Maybe I mis-use the word "weired".
  It made me think of that fim, the Substance, seeing myself crawling out of the back and jumping into a partly-new body. Is that still being myself? Probably, yes, or no. But more generally speaking, forming a mother language-self and English language-self. Maybe in the end, it's all just beacause the language barrier. I couldn't express some exact feelings by using the correct words.
  Two clocks live in my mind. One for homeland, one for the place I live presently. When I'm awake, they're sleeping. When they're awake, I'm sleeping. We are starting to walk the differnt path, but still, the same path to death.
  What will you think of "the west" before you living in the west and during you live in the west. To the American, they're from the west just moved to another country. Eventually, not that "being a foreigner in European countries". The language. The culture. The ego. What can you overcome and what can still maintain. The only distinctive aspect is you are an Asian.
  Trying to create in English is not so difficult as I imagine now, maybe I made numerous mistakes and couldn't be aware of them. Although writing in English provide a new opportunity to discover myself.
